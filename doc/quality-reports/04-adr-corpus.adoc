== Scope

`doc/adr/` holds 71 records (13,043 lines). Their filenames are the decision sentence, for example `0036-Forward_filtering_is_a_three-mode_policy_inside_an_authorised_route.adoc`. The template and rules are in `doc/development/adr-writing.adoc`:

* the skeleton is Status, Decision, Alternatives rejected, Consequences, Links;
* the template prescribes `:toc-title:` and `:source-highlighter:`;
* the hard ceiling is 500 body lines (`AdrRecordLengthContractTest`), and there is no smaller line target.

ADRs are point-in-time records, so slimming removes only material that is verbatim duplicated elsewhere.

== Rejected Findings of the Earlier Draft

* `<<_consequences>>` (0036), `<<_risks>>` (0048) and `<<_accepted_costs>>` (0057) resolve to auto-generated section IDs. `verify-links` reports them broken because it does not model auto-generated IDs.
* ADR-0075 records where resource-exhaustion detection is owned. It is not a test-naming convention and stays an ADR.
* No ADR carries a `// Maintain the metadata block` comment. The progressive-disclosure and authoring-discipline comments come from the template.
* The "we/us" hits in 0051 and 0054 are inside quotations.
